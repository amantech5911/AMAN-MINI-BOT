# Update 2.9.6 — .age calculator

## Naya: .age <janam ki tareekh> (tools)
- `.age 15/03/2000` — umar saal/mahine/din mein, kul mahine/hafte/din/ghante, janam ka din (weekday), zodiac sign, aur agli birthday mein kitne din baki (birthday aaj ho to mubarak ka message).
- Tareekh formats: `15/03/2000`, `15-3-2000`, `15.03.2000`, `2000-03-15`, `15 March 2000`, `March 15 2000`. Tareekh DD/MM/YYYY mani jati hai.
- Kisi khaas din ki umar: `.age 15/03/2000 30/06/2025`.
- Aaj ki tareekh Pakistan ke waqt (Asia/Karachi) se li jati hai, Railway ka UTC clock nahi.
- Alias: `.agecalc`, `.calcage`, `.umar`.

## Purani random .age
`new-pack.js` mein pehle `.age` ek random umar ka mazaak tha. Takrao se bachne ke liye wo ab `.ageguess` (alias `.agerandom`) hai.
